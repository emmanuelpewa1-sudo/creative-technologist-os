My CTOS Philosophy

CTOS stands for Creative Technologist Operating System.
It is a philosophy that guides my approach to learning and creating as a creative technologist.
The CTOS philosophy emphasizes the importance of continuous learning, experimentation, and collaboration in the pursuit of innovation and creativity.
By embracing the CTOS philosophy, I aim to develop a mindset that allows me to adapt to new technologies, explore new ideas, and create meaningful experiences that have a positive impact on society.
